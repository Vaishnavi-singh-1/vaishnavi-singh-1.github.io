Featured project

Demand Forecasting & Inventory Planning

Python (pandas) · Excel · Nov 2026

Many small and mid-sized manufacturers plan inventory with rules of thumb, like repeating last month's demand — causing stockouts on some items and cash tied up in others. I built an end-to-end planning model for 15 packaging SKUs on 36 months of demand to show how better forecasts cut inventory without hurting service.

Prepare data

36 months of simulated demand with trend, seasonality and noise; train/test split.

Forecast

Naive, moving average, seasonal naive, exponential smoothing, Holt-Winters — best per SKU by MAPE/WAPE.

Plan inventory

Safety stock and reorder points at a 95% service level.

Measure impact

Inventory under improved vs naive forecasts, in dollars.

Deliver

Python notebook, charts and an Excel plan a planner can use without code.
